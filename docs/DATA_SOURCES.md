# Source and licensing register

- **Natural Earth**, Admin 0 countries, 1:110m. [Source](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/), [public-domain terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Mirror and SHA-256 recorded in the manifest. Properties are reduced for the map; geometry is unmodified.
- **World Bank, World Development Indicators**, indicators NY.GDP.MKTP.CD, SP.POP.TOTL, NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG, NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS. [API documentation](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392), [license and additional terms](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses). Data are transformed into a common schema; original values and missingness retained. Credit World Bank on redistribution.

The manifest retrieval timestamps come from the download execution clock. They are not economic publication dates. Source update dates are recorded separately where supplied. No source contains historical vintages. Never use retrieval dates as a substitute for original releases.

The cache ends in 2024 deliberately to retain a common completed annual horizon. A later cache retrieval does not make 2024 observations current conditions.

- **US Treasury 2024 daily par yield curves:** official [XML feed documentation](https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed). Bundled XML contains 250 observed dates and 13 maturities (3,250 entries), normalized to `data/processed/treasury.parquet`; provenance/hash in `data/treasury-manifest.json`. US federal government data with attribution. Feed retrieved 2026-10-09; original publication dates and vintages are unknown. Par yields are distinct from spot rates and realized bond/ETF total returns.

- **OECD ICIO 2025 regular edition:** supplied authorized 2016–2022 ZIP plus ReadMe/annex. Seven validated five-sector aggregates (81 areas, 405 nodes) are bundled. Raw ZIP/member hashes, mapping and retained accounting exceptions are in `data/processed/network/metadata.json`; reproduction and attribution terms are in `OECD_IMPORT.md`. Source: [OECD ICIO](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/inter-country-input-output-tables.html), accessed 2026-10-09. Derived aggregation and scenario opinions are ATLAS work, not OECD endorsement. Downstream redistribution must preserve OECD attribution and that requirement for further recipients.
- **Current WDI:** separate latest completed-year 2025 snapshot: 660 rows, 592 nonmissing. Raw files and hashes are retained; the 1960–2024 research dataset remains frozen. Annual reference periods are not release dates. Same World Bank attribution terms apply.
- **Treasury continuation:** 2025/2026 XML plus the original 2024 cache provide 694 dates through 2026-10-09. New feeds include a six-week tenor; nulls remain explicit. `treasury-current.json` records exact hashes/retrieval dates. No real-time quote or spot-rate claim.
- **Official events:** [Federal Reserve monetary releases](https://www.federalreserve.gov/feeds/press_monetary.xml) and [ECB press RSS](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/rss/press.html). Thirty dated titles and original links are cached; article bodies are not redistributed. Latest cached announcement 2026-10-08; retrieval 2026-10-09. Limited source coverage, not comprehensive news. Explicit refresh uses certificate-validated HTTPS, fixed endpoints and a 5 MB response limit.
